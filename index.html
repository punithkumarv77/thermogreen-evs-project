System Architecture (Input → Processing → Output)

Designed for BCS508 Environmental Studies & E-Waste Management — Digital Solution Framework.

1 · Input

Multi-Source Thermal & GIS Data

  • Satellite Imagery: Landsat-8/9 Thermal Infrared (TIRS) & MODIS Land Surface Temperature.
  • IoT Micro-Sensors: Low-cost ESP32 + BME280 nodes measuring street-level air temp & humidity.
  • GIS & Land Use: Sentinel-2 NDVI vegetation index, building footprint & road asphalt maps.
  • Weather Feeds: IMD ambient temperature & rural baseline station data.
2 · Processing

Spatial Analytics & Cooling Model

  • UHI Calculation: Computes ΔT = Turban − Trural per 500m grid cell.
  • Hotspot Clustering: Identifies severe thermal islands using spatial clustering (DBSCAN).
  • ML Regression Engine: Predicts ΔT reduction from canopy, cool roofs, and permeable surfaces.
  • Energy & Carbon Estimator: Converts cooling gains into kWh AC savings and CO₂ avoided.
3 · Output

Interactive Decision Support

  • Color-Coded Thermal Map: Real-time neighborhood hotspot visualization for citizens & planners.
  • Cooling Simulator: What-if intervention planner for municipal corporations & campuses.
  • Heat Advisories: Profile-specific health alerts for workers, elderly, and students.
  • Priority Action Reports: Ranked zones for urban forestry and cool-roof subsidies.

1. Lower Urban Peak Temperatures

Guides targeted tree plantation and cool-roof deployment to cut localized surface temperatures by 2°C–4.5°C.

2. Reduced AC Energy & Carbon Emissions

Cooler micro-climates lower peak summer cooling electricity demand, directly cutting thermal power CO₂ emissions.

3. Improved Public Health & Safety

Early hotspot warnings protect outdoor workers, elderly citizens, and children from heatstroke and dehydration.

4. Urban Biodiversity & Stormwater Relief

Promotes native tree corridors and permeable pavements that recharge groundwater and support urban wildlife.

5. UN SDG Alignment

Directly advances SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities), SDG 13 (Climate Action), SDG 7 (Clean Energy), and SDG 3 (Health).
